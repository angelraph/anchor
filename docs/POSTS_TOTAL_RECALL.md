## LinkedIn

**I let the blockchain decide whether I kept my word.**

This morning I told my accountability bot: "I'll save 1 USDsui by Friday." Then I put 1 USDC on it: keep the promise and the money comes back, miss it and it goes to a friend.

Forty minutes later, without me pressing anything, the bot messaged me: "Verified on Sui: balance went from 2 to 3.1 USDsui. Kept." A second later my 1 USDC was on its way back. No human decided. My wallet did.

That's Anchor, the project I've been building for the Total Recall hackathon by Tatum and Walrus Foundation.

**The problem**

Most people don't lack goals; they lack follow-through. We make promises to ourselves every day and quietly let them slide. Reminder apps fire once and get dismissed. AI assistants forget you when the chat ends. Coaches work, but they're expensive.

**What Anchor does**

Anchor is an accountability partner that lives in Telegram and remembers you.

• You tell it what you'll do and by when. It stores the promise as encrypted long-term memory on Walrus, in a namespace that belongs only to you.
• It messages you first: a heads-up before the deadline, a check at the deadline, and a weekly summary of your follow-through.
• It learns your patterns. If late nights on your phone keep derailing your mornings, it remembers, and the next time you plan, it suggests what actually worked for you before.

**What's new: promises the blockchain can verify**

For money promises, your word shouldn't be the evidence. Your wallet should.

• Link a Sui wallet. Anchor can read it, never move it.
• Say "I'll save 50 USDC by Friday" or "I'll pay Tobi back 20 USDC". Anchor reads your wallet through Tatum's Sui RPC and closes the promise itself when the evidence shows up.
• Optionally stake money on it. Keep your word and it comes back; miss it and it goes to someone you chose. Anchor never keeps the money.
• Each week can become a signed report stored on Walrus: portable proof of follow-through that anyone can verify.
• With your permission, AI agents can pay a cent per read (over x402, settled on Sui) for that signed record. They see counts and proofs, never your conversations.
• The same memory works in Claude Code and other AI tools through MCP.

**What I built for Total Recall**

Everything on-chain above was built in the hackathon window: chain-verified promises through Tatum's Sui RPC, stakes in USDC or USDsui, x402 agent payments, signed reports on Walrus, and portable memory over MCP. It all ran live on Sui mainnet today, with the transactions public: wallet proof, a real stake that was kept and paid back, an AI agent paying for a record, and a signed report certified on Walrus.

It sits on Anchor's memory core, which people already use every day: 15 people and 169+ memories on Walrus mainnet.
